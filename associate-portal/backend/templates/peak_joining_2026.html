One Motion Driver Joining Incentive Agreement

2026 Peak Joining Incentive Agreement · Private & Confidential

1. Joining Bonus

The Driver will be eligible for a joining bonus of up to £1,500, subject to completing the 8-week qualifying period. The amount payable will depend on the number of qualifying conditions met, as set out in section 5.

2. Qualifying Period

The qualifying period is 8 weeks from the date of a driver’s first day on route.

3. Qualifying Conditions

The following four conditions will be assessed over the 8-week qualifying period to determine the amount of the joining bonus payable:

Performance measureRequired standard
Delivery PerformanceA POD (Photo on Delivery Compliance) performance score of 98.50% or above.
Combined ServiceA performance score of 98% for (DCR) Delivery Completion Rate of 98.60% and (DSC) Delivery Success Conditions of Less than 1,250.
Working daysAn average of at least 5 working days per week across the qualifying period.
Parcel Claims or LossesNo upheld parcel claims relating to parcels handled by the Driver with no reported LOR (Lost on Road) instances, and DNR (Customer Did Not Receive Concessions) Score of below 1200 average across the qualifying period.

4. Parcel Claims or Loss Review Period

Following completion of the 8-week qualifying period, a further 3 week parcel claims or loss review period will apply. This allows time for delayed parcel claims relating to parcels handled by the Driver during the qualifying period to be reported and reviewed.

Any such claims reported during the qualifying period or before the end of the claims review period will be taken into account when determining whether the parcel claims or loss condition has been met.

Where a relevant parcel claim remains outstanding at the end of the claims review period, payment will be deferred until the claim has been investigated and its outcome confirmed.

For the purposes of this agreement, the no parcel claims condition means that no relevant parcel claims have been upheld. A claim that is rejected or withdrawn will not count as a failure of this condition.

5. Assessment and Payment

Performance against the qualifying conditions will be assessed upon completion of the 8-week qualifying period, with the parcel claims assessment finalised following the claims or loss review period.

Subject to the Driver completing the 8-week qualifying period, the amount of the joining bonus payable will be determined by the number of qualifying conditions met.

Where all four conditions are met, the Driver will receive 100% of the joining bonus (£1,500). Where exactly three conditions are met, 50% (£750) will be payable. Where exactly two conditions are met, 25% (£375) will be payable. Where exactly one condition is met, 10% (£150) will be payable. No bonus will be payable where none of the qualifying conditions are met.

Payment will be made following completion of the parcel claims or loss review period and resolution of any outstanding relevant claims. An upheld relevant parcel claim will mean that the parcel claims condition has not been met, and the amount payable will be determined by how many of the remaining qualifying conditions have been met.
